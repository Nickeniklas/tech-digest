Lead Story

Microsoft is reportedly giving up on the personal AI chatbot race

Bloomberg reports that Microsoft is abandoning its push to compete as a personal, consumer AI chatbot and is rebooting Copilot instead.

What this means

If you use Copilot at home or at work, expect it to change shape rather than disappear. It is also a sign that the big consumer chatbot market is settling around fewer players — worth keeping in mind before you build habits or workflows around any single assistant.

▸ Excel now lets one cell hold several values Microsoft 365 Insider Blog

Microsoft is adding lists and arrays to Excel, so a single cell can contain multiple values instead of one. If you keep tags, attendees or product options in a spreadsheet, you may no longer need to spread them across extra columns or cram them into comma-separated text.

▸ Jury finds Facebook liable in the Cambridge Analytica case CBS News

A jury found Facebook liable for deceiving its users over the Cambridge Analytica data scandal. For anyone who handles customer data — marketers and lawyers especially — it is a reminder that how you describe your data practices can carry legal weight years later.

VIA CBS News ↗

▸ Court upholds Pentagon's label of Anthropic as a supply chain risk CNBC

A U.S. appeals court upheld the Pentagon's designation of Anthropic, the company behind Claude, as a supply chain risk. If your organisation works with the U.S. government, this can affect which AI tools you are allowed to use on that work.

VIA CNBC ↗

▸ Camera data put an innocent woman in jail for 13 days Jezebel

A single piece of data from Flock, a network of automated licence-plate cameras, led to an innocent woman spending 13 days in jail, and she is now suing. It shows what happens when automated surveillance is treated as proof rather than a lead.

VIA Jezebel ↗

GitHub rewrote Copilot's engine in Rust — with Copilot

GitHub ported the runtime that powers its Copilot coding agent to 800,000 lines of production Rust, using Copilot's own agents to do much of the work. GitHub says a rewrite of this size wasn't affordable before agents existed.

Why it matters

Large language migrations are among the most expensive, least loved jobs in software. This is a concrete, large-scale data point that AI agents are changing which rewrites teams can justify.
