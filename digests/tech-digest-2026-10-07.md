## Claude Sonnet 5.5: faster and cheaper than the model it replaces

**What happened**
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in its Claude 5.5 family. The company says it is a clear upgrade over Sonnet 5, runs more than 30% faster, and costs up to 30% less for most work.

**What this means**
If you use Claude for drafting, research or summarising, you should get answers sooner and your team's AI bill may shrink. Sonnet is the middle tier most people use day to day, so this is the upgrade you are most likely to notice.

Source: [Anthropic ↗](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5)

## Quick Hits

### Utah will let AI examine patients and prescribe without human oversight

TechSpot reports that Utah is set to become the first US state to let an AI system examine patients and prescribe medication with no human reviewing the decision. If you work in healthcare, insurance or law, this is a test case for who is responsible when an AI makes a medical call.

Source: [TechSpot ↗](https://www.techspot.com/news/114111-utah-become-first-state-ai-examine-patients-prescribe.html)

### South Korea says AI agents appear to have been used to hack its banks

South Korea's president said AI agents — AI systems that can carry out tasks on their own — appear to have been used in attacks on the country's banks, Reuters reports. It is one of the first times a government has publicly linked a major cyberattack to AI agents.

Source: [Reuters ↗](https://www.reuters.com/world/south-koreas-lee-says-ai-appears-have-been-used-bank-hacks-2026-10-06/)

### OpenAI shares progress on AI doing new mathematics

OpenAI published an update on its AI's work in mathematics, alongside a preprint claiming a faster method for multiplying very large whole numbers. If it holds up to expert review, it would be a rare case of AI contributing a genuinely new result rather than repeating known ones.

Source: [OpenAI ↗](https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/)

## Under the Hood

### GitHub's open-source AI agent finds 24 Android vulnerabilities

**What happened**
GitHub's security team used an open-source AI security agent, run as a series of targeted tasks, to find 24 vulnerabilities in Android apps, including critical bugs. GitHub has published how the taskflows work and how to run the same agent on your own app.

**Why it matters**
Security reviews are slow and expensive; an agent that can be pointed at an app and surface real bugs lowers the bar for smaller teams. Because it is open source, defenders can inspect and adapt it — useful given today's reports of attackers using AI agents too.

Source: [GitHub Blog ↗](https://github.blog/security/how-we-found-24-android-vulnerabilities-using-our-open-source-ai-security-agent/)

### Small "decision models" keep arriving: Strands Decider 2B and OpenAI's Decisions API

**What happened**
Strands released Decider 2B, a small open-source model built to choose between actions rather than write long answers, and OpenAI put its Decisions API into public beta. Both target the same job: picking the next step inside an automated workflow.

**Why it matters**
Most agent steps are small choices — which tool to call, which branch to take. A 2-billion-parameter model is far cheaper to run than a frontier model, so routing those choices to a small specialist can cut costs and latency for anyone building agents.

Source: [Strands Agents ↗](https://strandsagents.com/blog/introducing-strands-decider/)
