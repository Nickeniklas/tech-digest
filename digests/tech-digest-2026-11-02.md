## Postgres 19 is running late — because AI keeps finding bugs

**What happened**
Robert Haas, a long-time developer of PostgreSQL — the free database behind countless websites and business apps — writes that the next release, Postgres 19, is delayed because far more bugs are being reported than before. His post's title puts the cause plainly: it's AI's fault.

**What this means**
AI tools are now turning up problems in software faster than the people who maintain it can fix them. For you, that likely means more, and more frequent, security and bug-fix updates from the software you depend on — and some releases arriving later than planned.

Source: [Robert Haas (blog) ↗](http://rhaas.blogspot.com/2026/10/postgres-19-is-late-and-its-ais-fault.html)

### Lenovo's 'encrypted' fingerprint USB drive is just an SD card in a case

**What happened**
A widely shared report says a Lenovo USB drive marketed as encrypted and unlocked by fingerprint turns out to be an ordinary, unencrypted SD memory card inside a casing.

**Why it matters**
A fingerprint reader only helps if the data behind it is actually scrambled. If you carry sensitive files — client records, student data, legal documents — on 'secure' drives, don't rely on the label alone.
