## Liquid AI releases LFM3: one family of small models for text, images and audio

**What happened**
Liquid AI released LFM3, a new generation of its foundation models that puts text, vision, audio, combined 'omni' and decision-making models on one shared base. Every model is around 2 billion parameters — small enough to run on ordinary hardware — and you pick the one for your type of data, then fine-tune it for your task.

**What this means**
AI is moving from one giant all-purpose assistant toward small, specialised models built for a single job, like transcribing calls or reading forms. That can mean cheaper tools that run on your own device, keeping data private — useful for anyone in law, healthcare or education handling sensitive material.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/lfm3)

## Under the Hood

### Can AI judge whether a research idea is actually new?

**What happened**
The Allen Institute for AI released 'Old Ideas, Novel Problems', a study of how reliably large language models can judge the novelty of scientific research ideas. Alongside the paper, it published a benchmark, data and code for testing these AI 'novelty judges', plus an automated pipeline researchers can use to build their own.

**Why it matters**
AI is increasingly used to screen papers, grant proposals and ideas. Before trusting it to say what's new, you need a way to measure how often it gets that call wrong — this benchmark gives researchers and reviewers that yardstick.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/novelty-bench)

### Python's experimental speed-up compiler is being removed

**What happened**
LWN reports that Python's experimental JIT compiler — a feature that translates code into faster machine instructions while a program runs — is set to be removed from the language.

**Why it matters**
The JIT was one of the main bets for making Python, the language behind most AI tooling, run faster. Its removal means developers shouldn't count on that route to speed-ups and will keep relying on other approaches.

Source: [LWN ↗](https://lwn.net/SubscriberLink/1102102/0c00e4d550f25f8c/)
