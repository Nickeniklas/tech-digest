## Claude gets a zero data retention option for enterprises, now in beta

**What happened**
Anthropic has put zero data retention with Enterprise Frontier Safeguards into beta. Zero data retention means the prompts and answers your organisation sends through Claude are not stored once the request is handled.

**What this means**
If you work with client files, patient records or unreleased plans, data retention is often what keeps AI tools off-limits. Lawyers, healthcare staff and finance teams should ask their IT department whether this option changes what they are allowed to use Claude for.

Source: [Claude ↗](https://claude.com/resources/articles/zero-data-retention-with-enterprise-frontier-safeguards)

### Stalwart now works as a drop-in replacement for Microsoft Exchange

Stalwart, an open-source mail server, says it can now stand in directly for Microsoft Exchange, the system many organisations run their email and calendars on. For schools, nonprofits and small firms, that opens a possible route away from Microsoft licensing costs.

Source: [Stalwart ↗](https://stalw.art/blog/microsoft-exchange-replacement/)

### Ladybird, a web browser built from scratch, reaches alpha

Ladybird, an independent browser that doesn't reuse Chrome's or Firefox's engine, is now in alpha — an early version for testing. Almost every browser today runs on technology from Google, Apple or Mozilla, so a genuinely new one is rare.

Source: [Ladybird ↗](https://ladybird.org/posts/alpha/)

### A 27-billion-parameter AI model shrunk into a 10 GB file

Nari Labs released NariQ, a compressed version of the open Qwen3.8-27B model that fits into a 10 GB file while claiming quality close to the full-precision original. Smaller files mean capable AI can run on an ordinary laptop, keeping your data on your own machine.

Source: [Hugging Face ↗](https://huggingface.co/nari-labs/Qwen3.8-27B-NariQ-GGUF)

## Under the Hood

### How to build an evaluation that keeps up with AI agents

**What happened**
Hugging Face published a guide to building and maintaining agentic evaluations — tests that give an AI agent tasks, check its answers or actions, and produce a score. The post stresses that the hard work is defining tasks that reflect real domain expertise and scoring them reliably over time.

**Why it matters**
Evaluations shape which AI systems get built, adopted and trusted. If you are choosing an agent for your team, a well-maintained evaluation built on your own tasks tells you far more than a vendor's headline benchmark.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/agentic-evals)

### RoboTrack: teaching robots to keep their eye on the object

**What happened**
AllenAI introduced RoboTrack, a point-tracking system for real-world robotic manipulation. To pick something up, a robot must follow the positions of both its gripper and the object as they move — for example, keeping track of a pipette tip while lining it up in a lab.

**Why it matters**
Reliable perception through a whole interaction is one of the main gaps between robots that work in demos and robots that work in labs, warehouses and hospitals.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/robotrack)
