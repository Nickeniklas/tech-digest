Lead Story

NVIDIA Open-Sources Its Platform for Building Physical AI

NVIDIA published Cosmos, a free open platform containing world models, datasets, and tools for building AI that operates in the physical world — robots, self-driving vehicles, and smart infrastructure. Any developer or researcher can now use these foundational building blocks.

Use CaseWhat Cosmos Provides
RoboticsPhysics-aware world models for training
Autonomous VehiclesSimulation datasets for self-driving systems
Smart InfrastructureTools for AI that monitors real environments
ResearchPre-built world models and open datasets
What this means

Until now, the technology underlying physical AI required enormous proprietary research investment. Cosmos makes that foundation open, which could accelerate development of autonomous systems across industries from logistics to construction — and brings the robotics era closer to reality.

▸ A Free Self-Hosted Alternative to Google's NotebookLM Is Now Available GitHub – lfnovo/open-notebook

Open-Notebook replicates what Google NotebookLM does — letting you chat with your own documents and generate audio overviews — but runs entirely on your own hardware with no data sent to Google. It adds features the official version doesn't have yet, and keeps your documents private.

VIA GitHub – lfnovo/open-notebook ↗

▸ GitHub Releases an Official SDK for Embedding Copilot Into Any App GitHub – github/copilot-sdk

GitHub published a multi-platform SDK that lets developers integrate Copilot Agent — its AI coding assistant — directly into third-party tools and services. Products built on GitHub can now add native AI coding capabilities rather than building them from scratch.

VIA GitHub – github/copilot-sdk ↗

▸ A Powerful Free OCR Tool Now Feeds Documents Directly Into AI GitHub – PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR

PaddleOCR converts PDFs, scanned pages, and images into structured text that AI systems can process — supporting over 100 languages. For teams dealing with large document archives, it removes a key bottleneck between paper records and AI analysis tools.

Deep Dive

A New AI Research Skill Searches Six Platforms in Parallel

Developer mvanhorn published last30days-skill, a reusable skill for AI agent frameworks that takes any topic, searches Reddit, X, YouTube, Hacker News, Polymarket, and the general web simultaneously, then synthesizes results into a single grounded summary with contradictions flagged across sources.

SourceType of Signal
RedditCommunity discussion & sentiment
X (Twitter)Real-time reactions & announcements
YouTubeVideo coverage & tutorials
Hacker NewsTechnical community analysis
PolymarketPrediction market odds
WebNews articles & official sources
Why it matters

Most AI research tools query one source at a time. Running searches in parallel and cross-checking results across different community types — technical forums, social media, prediction markets — produces a more complete and less biased picture. It's designed as a composable skill: drop it into any agent framework and get cross-platform research on demand.
