## Meta releases Muse Spark Open, a free-to-download AI model

**What happened**
Meta published Muse Spark Open, an open-weight AI model — the full model files are free to download — that understands text, images and audio and can work with long documents. Hugging Face has added support so developers can run and customise it with popular tools, from cloud servers to Macs.

**What this means**
Organisations that can't send sensitive material to an outside AI service — law firms, schools, hospitals — get another capable option they can run on their own systems. Expect it to show up inside apps and tools you already use.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/meta-muse-spark-open)

## Quick Hits

### Meta's new terms let it scrape business websites from October 30

Nieman Lab reports that Meta's new commercial terms mean business accounts will consent to Meta scraping their websites starting October 30. Legal experts say this could cover news sites too. If your organisation runs a Facebook or Instagram business page, it's worth having someone read the new terms before that date.

Source: [Nieman Lab ↗](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/metas-new-commercial-terms-will-allow-the-scraping-of-news-sites-legal-experts-say/)

### Targeted ads used to deliver spyware with zero clicks

Researchers at Citizen Lab describe how a company called Discover Tech turned targeted online ads into a delivery route for mercenary spyware. "Zero-click" means the victim didn't have to tap anything for their device to be infected. It's a reminder that keeping your phone and browser updated matters even if you never click suspicious links.

Source: [Citizen Lab ↗](https://citizenlab.ca/research/how-discover-tech-turned-targeted-ads-into-a-pipeline-for-spyware/)

### Krea 3: an AI image model built for editing

Krea released Krea 3, an image model designed for editing pictures at scale rather than only creating new ones from scratch. For designers and marketing teams, that points to faster batch work — adjusting many product shots or campaign images at once.

Source: [Krea ↗](https://www.krea.ai/krea-3)

### Internet routes are now 70% protected against hijacking

IPinfo reports that RPKI, a system that verifies who is allowed to direct internet traffic to a given address, now protects 70% of internet routes. Separately, the international body that keeps official time decided to stop adding leap seconds to world clock time (UTC). Both are quiet plumbing changes that make the internet more reliable.

Source: [IPinfo ↗](https://ipinfo.io/blog/rpki-protects-70-internet-routes)

## Under the Hood

### IBM's Granite Switch 4.1: chat, decisions and speech from one endpoint

**What happened**
IBM released Granite Switch 4.1, a modular family of models where add-ons (adapters) give one base model extra skills. The new version adds a Decision adapter for quick yes/no-style judgments and what IBM calls its fastest speech-recognition model, all served from a single endpoint.

**Why it matters**
Instead of running separate models for chatting, classifying and transcribing, teams can run one and switch skills as needed — simpler to deploy and cheaper to host. It also fits the recent trend toward small, fast "decision models."

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-switch-4-1)

### NVIDIA opens a 2.8-trillion-token dataset for Indian languages

**What happened**
NVIDIA published a 2.8-trillion-token training corpus for Indic (Indian) languages, plus the pipeline used to create it: English source documents are turned into Indic training data through a twelve-language workflow and a ten-language workflow. NVIDIA used it to extend the training of its Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model.

**Why it matters**
Most AI training data is English, so models are weaker in widely spoken languages like Hindi or Tamil. Releasing both the data and the recipe lets others build or adapt it for their own languages.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/scaling-indic-pretraining)
