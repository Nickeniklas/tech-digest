## A small, cheap AI model beats the giants at pulling data out of documents

**What happened**
Dharma AI released Dharma Field Extractor, a compact model that reads any type of document and returns exactly the fields you ask for — names, dates, amounts — in a structured format. The company says it outperformed every leading large AI model it tested, at a fraction of the cost.

**What this means**
If your work involves copying details out of contracts, invoices, forms or applications, this kind of tool is getting cheaper and more accurate fast. Lawyers, HR teams and office managers are the obvious beneficiaries — though it's worth checking results on your own documents before trusting them.

Source: [Hugging Face Blog ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/Dharma-AI/dharma-field-extractor)

## Under the Hood

### Anthropic reports progress on provable AI inference

**What happened**
Anthropic published research on 'provable inference' for large language models. Inference is the moment a model generates an answer; making it provable means being able to verify that a specific answer really came from a specific model, run as claimed.

**Why it matters**
As AI answers feed into legal, financial and medical decisions, being able to prove which model produced an output — and that it wasn't swapped or tampered with — matters for audits, compliance and trust between companies that buy AI from providers.

Source: [Anthropic ↗](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference)

### Qualcomm's Cloud AI 100 Ultra: a 150-watt card for running AI models

**What happened**
Qualcomm's Cloud AI 100 Ultra is an accelerator card — a plug-in chip board for servers — built to run AI models. It packs 128GB of memory at 548GB/s of bandwidth while drawing 150 watts.

**Why it matters**
Memory size and speed decide how large a model a single card can run and how quickly it answers. A low-power card with this much memory gives companies another option besides NVIDIA for running AI in their own data centres.

Source: [Qualcomm ↗](https://www.qualcomm.com/data-center/products/cloud-ai-100-ultra)
