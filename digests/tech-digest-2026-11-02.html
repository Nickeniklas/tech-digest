AI has become so good at finding bugs that one of the world's most widely used databases is shipping its next version late.

Lead Story

Postgres 19 is running late — because AI keeps finding bugs

Robert Haas, a long-time developer of PostgreSQL — the free database behind countless websites and business apps — writes that the next release, Postgres 19, is delayed because far more bugs are being reported than before. His post's title puts the cause plainly: it's AI's fault.

What this means

AI tools are now turning up problems in software faster than the people who maintain it can fix them. For you, that likely means more, and more frequent, security and bug-fix updates from the software you depend on — and some releases arriving later than planned.
