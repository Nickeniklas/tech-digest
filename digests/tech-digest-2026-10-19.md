## Anthropic expands its program for verified security professionals

**What happened**
Anthropic launched an expanded Cyber Verification Program, which gives qualifying security professionals access to Claude's advanced cyber capabilities with fewer automatic blocks. The program now has three access tiers, and security teams apply for the level that fits their work.

**What this means**
AI companies are moving from blocking risky security requests for everyone to granting more power to people they have checked. If you work in IT, risk or compliance, expect your security team or vendors to start using AI tools that can do more serious defensive work — and expect questions about who gets verified.

### Two bugs in Broadcom WiFi code affect many routers

A security researcher found two flaws in a single piece of Broadcom WiFi software that ships inside many different routers, running on more than one operating system. Because the same code is reused across brands, one fix depends on many manufacturers — a good reminder to keep your home and office router updated.

Source: [Quentin Kaiser ↗](https://quentinkaiser.be/security/2026/10/16/one-parser-two-bugs/)

## Under the Hood

### Hybrid reasoning agents: think silently, act in words

**What happened**
A new research paper proposes AI agents that do their reasoning in latent space — the model's internal numerical representation — instead of writing out every step as text, and only switch to text when they act.

**Why it matters**
Today's reasoning models spell out long chains of thought, which costs time and money. Reasoning internally could make agents faster and cheaper, but it also makes their thinking harder for people to read and check — a trade-off that matters for anyone auditing AI decisions.

Source: [arXiv ↗](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861)
